The prints in `prints/` are not in the game at the moment. They were the
scenery for a while in September 2026, but twelve prints in the same warm
light lost the feeling of travelling from morning to night that the drawn
scenery gives, so the drawn scenery came back (redrawn, by the scripts in
`tools/scenery/`). The prints are kept for pictures on the scrolls of river
events, tombs and oases. Where Roberts drew no view of a stop, the table says
what the print really shows. Each is trimmed to the picture (no page margins,
captions or colour strips) and resized.
